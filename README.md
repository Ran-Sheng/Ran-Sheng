## About

I am a PhD student in the joint doctoral program between the **Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA)** and **Renmin University of China**, in the group *Theory and Computation of PDE*.

My research sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, scientific computing, and machine learning. I work on **multiscale theory**, the **forward and inverse problems of differential equations**, and learning-based methods for solving and inferring across scales.
